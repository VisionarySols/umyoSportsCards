UMYO Sports and Zae CPE Partner with Kenyon Glover to Revolutionize the Entertainment Industry

Dallas, TX — UMYO Sports, co-founded by a dynamic hip-hop artist and media innovator Zae CPE, is proud to announce a groundbreaking partnership with actor, producer, and entrepreneur Kenyon Glover (KG). This strategic alliance aims to provide a vibrant and inclusive hub where artists, producers, agents, directors, advertisers, and other industry professionals can brand, network, and collaborate under one digital roof

UMYO Sports is redefining talent discovery with a cutting-edge platform that allows users to search for talent based on music genre, location, and performance metrics, creating a new era of visibility and opportunity for artists of all kinds. From unsigned musical talents to rising filmmakers, the platform offers a community-first approach—where creatives can upload video reels, content, engage in contests, and connect with professionals across the entertainment spectrum.

Zae CPE, a prodigy in the media space since age 12, began his career with STI740—a youth-led media show where he worked in front of and behind the camera. Inspired by that experience and fueled by family support, he and Kenyon Glover launched UMYO as a safe, empowering space for creative voices. The platform features interactive contests where fans can vote for "Talent of the Month" either for free or through paid voting. Artists receive 80% of paid votes, with 20% allocated to platform hosting costs, ensuring creators are directly rewarded for their work.

“This is more than just an app or a site—it’s an entertainment ecosystem,” said Zae CPE.

About Zae CPE

A visionary artist and entrepreneur known for his raw lyrical energy and deeply personal storytelling. From heartbreak to hustle, his music blends introspection with impact. As founder of UMYO Ent, Zae leads the charge in reimagining entertainment for the next generation.

About Kenyon Glover

A multi-talented actor, director, author, and former pro athlete who has turned personal challenges into purpose-driven success. His credits span film, TV, radio, fashion, and fitness. A true mogul in the making, Glover is committed to uplifting talent and creating change across industries. With over 69 acting credits, 23 producer credits, and 6 directorial projects, he is committed to uplifting emerging talent.

The Future of Entertainment

“UMYO is the future—a tech-driven revolution in how big brother collaborates with younger brother in an entertainment world that discovers and supports talent,” said Kenyon Glover.

Together, Zae CPE and Kenyon Glover aim to build a positive, inclusive, and tech-forward environment for artists, agencies, and fans alike—bridging gaps and breaking barriers across the industry.
