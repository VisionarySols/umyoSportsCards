Welcome to Revolution 00 — Where Ownership Begins

Revolution 00 was built for us, by us — a digital nation where people of color can thrive without the exploitation, censorship, or chaos found on mainstream platforms. Here, we don’t just talk about change — we’ve built the infrastructure to create it. Whether you're an entrepreneur, student, creator, or community leader, Revolution 00 is your space to connect, grow, and circulate wealth within trusted, private digital ecosystems.

We believe in action, not permission. That’s why we’ve created secure tools modeled after some of the most popular online platforms — but reimagined with our communities in mind. For just $9.99 a month, members gain access to livestreaming, webinars, secure messaging, e-commerce, and community-driven tools that support local businesses and mental wellness — all in a gated digital community built for growth, trust, and positivity.

What sets us apart?

We give back — consistently and intentionally. Every week, we support members with car payments, rent relief, food and gas assistance, HBCU scholarships, youth programs, and free promotion for small businesses. We believe generosity is currency — and that circulating resources within our own communities is how we build long-term strength and security.

Our tech partner, UMYO Wall Street Of Technology, heard our vision and made it a reality — creating everything we needed within weeks, not months. That means we don’t have to worry about complicated tech or high costs each month. They handle the backend as we build our future. Together, we share profits and power that puts culture first and gives leaders the freedom to lead.

This isn’t just a platform. It’s a revolution in how we engage, earn, support, and rise. So if you're ready to own your presence, amplify your voice, and be part of a community that stands on business — welcome home. Revolution 00 is where culture, commerce, and community meet — and where our future begins.
