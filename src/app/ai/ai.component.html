Men No Pause

Men United: Share. Heal. Build. Wednesdays @ 7:30 PM | Live Virtual Room
https://live.umyoconvo.site/rooms/menunited/join

A safe, powerful space for men of all ages to come together, reflect, and rise. Whether you’re 18 or 80, Men United is where real stories are told, hard lessons are shared, and purpose is reclaimed.

  • For the Young: Learn from seasoned men who’ve walked the road before you. Get life advice, career guidance, and mentorship from Black and Brown men who’ve made it—and made mistakes.
  • For the Grown: Pass the torch. Share your wisdom, experiences, and support. Be part of the collective upliftment of our community.
  • For the Brotherhood: This is your tribe. We network. We collaborate. We rise—together.
Weekly Format:

Create. Open the session with a powerful prompt or question—spark the conversation.

Engage. Authentic discussions, breakout circles, or 1:1 storytelling to share lived experiences.

Innovate. Leave with a tool, plan, or connection. Every week offers something actionable—whether it’s mental wellness tips, entrepreneurial resources, or support circles.
