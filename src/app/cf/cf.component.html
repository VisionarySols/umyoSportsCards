Two Brothers. One Purpose. A Community Reborn.

A Movement. A Platform. A Future for People of Color.

Phoenix, Arizona — In a world where technology too often divides us and politics continues to fail the communities most in need, two brothers are rewriting the story — not just for themselves, but for every Black and Brown person who has ever felt unheard, unseen, or left behind.

Born from necessity and driven by purpose, Revolution 00 is more than a platform. It is the heartbeat of a movement. It was born out of a shared pain, a relentless hope, and the unshakable belief that the power to change our reality has always lived within us.

Together, these two brothers looked around at their neighborhoods and saw something the world didn’t: potential still burning beneath the ashes of generational struggle.

“Nobody’s coming to save us,” says Ken. “But what if we could give our people the tools to save themselves? Not just inspiration — but infrastructure.”

Brothers by Blood. Warriors by Purpose - From Vision To Reality — Thanks to UMYO

Founded by Ken Muhammad and his younger brother King David, Revolution 00 didn’t begin in a boardroom. It started in living rooms filled with tough conversations, in barbershops where wisdom was passed down, and in community spaces where grief and hope lived side by side.

The vision became possible when Kenneth Muhammad saw Gregory Barnard’s story in Black Enterprise—a story of a Black father and his three sons building a tech legacy through UMYO, The Wall Street of Technology.

Kenneth reached out. Gregory listened. “Within weeks, not months, Gregory & his sons built exactly what we needed,” says Muhammad. “UMYO handles every tech concern. We don’t have to worry about hiring engineers or paying thousands monthly just to maintain it. They created the infrastructure, and now we share profits as real partners.”
