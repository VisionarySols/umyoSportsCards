Welcome to UMYO Military — Where Athletes Own the Game

UMYO Military is a next-generation digital platform built exclusively for athletes of all levels — from high school standouts to pro legends. Designed with sports in mind, UMYO Military provides a secure and empowering space where athletes can train, earn, connect, and grow — with the support of businesses that believe in their future.

This isn’t just about stats and highlights — it’s about ownership, opportunity, and community. Whether you’re a player, coach, content creator, or sports entrepreneur, UMYO Military equips you with the tools to level up your game — on and off the field.

For just $9.99/month, members get access to:

  • Athlete-focused livestreaming and exclusive sports content
  • Custom e-commerce stores for merch, gear, and digital products
  • Secure messaging with fans, brands, and peers
  • Weekly prize giveaways, business vouchers, and scholarships
  • Athlete marketing tools and monetization features
  • A gated community where athletes control their brand and platform

Why UMYO Military?

Because we believe in investing where it counts — our athletes. Each week, we directly support the sports community through:

  • Cash scholarships for students attending HBCUs and other universities
  • Rent, training, and travel support for rising stars
  • Gift cards and vouchers for gear from Black-owned and athlete-backed brands
  • Free promotion for athlete-owned businesses and creative projects

Business owners and sponsors can get involved starting at just $19.99/month — with branding, athlete partnerships, and premium promotional packages available during registration.

Our tech partner, UMYO Wall Street of Technology, powers the platform — so athletes can focus on winning, creating, and building their legacy. From livestreams to store sales to mentorship programs, we make it simple to thrive in one trusted place.

This is more than a platform — it’s a movement for athletic excellence, empowerment, and economic circulation. If you're ready to grow your fanbase, monetize your talent, and connect with a powerful network that champions you — UMYO Military is your arena.
