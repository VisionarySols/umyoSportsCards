Welcome to UMYO Ent Hub — Where Creatives Own the Game

UMYO Ent Hub is a next-generation digital platform built exclusively for artists, entertainers, and creatives of all kinds. From musicians and dancers to filmmakers and content creators, UMYO Ent Hub offers a secure and empowering space for you to train, earn, connect, and grow — with the backing of businesses that believe in your future.

This isn’t just about performances and releases — it’s about ownership, opportunity, and community. Whether you’re an artist, producer, content creator, or entertainment entrepreneur, UMYO Ent Hub equips you with the tools you need to level up your game — in the studio, on stage, or behind the scenes.

For just $9.99/month, members gain access to:

  • Creative Livestreaming and Exclusive Content
  • Custom E-commerce Stores for selling merch, music, and digital products
  • Secure Messaging to connect with fans, brands, and fellow creatives
  • Weekly Prize Giveaways, Business Vouchers, and Scholarships
  • Artist Marketing Tools and Monetization Features
  • A Gated Community where you control your brand and platform

Why UMYO Entertainment Hub?

We believe in investing where it counts — our creatives. Every week, we support the entertainment community through:

  • Cash scholarships for aspiring artists attending creative institutions
  • Rent, travel, and studio support for emerging talent
  • Gift cards and vouchers for gear from Black-owned and artist-backed brands
  • Free promotion for artist-owned businesses and creative projects

Business owners and sponsors can get involved starting at just $19.99/month — with branding, artist partnerships, and premium promotional packages available during registration.

Our tech partner, UMYO Wall Street of Technology, powers the platform — so artists can focus on creating, performing, and building their legacy. From livestreams and digital sales to mentorship programs and exclusive networking opportunities, we make it simple to thrive in one trusted space.

This is more than a platform — it’s a movement for creative excellence, empowerment, and economic growth. If you're ready to expand your fanbase, monetize your talent, and connect with a powerful network that champions you —UMYO Ent Hub is your stage.
